Sources, images & practice notes

These are original model reviews about real works, written for vocabulary practice. Evaluative opinions are examples, not the learner’s personal views or quoted professional reviews. Brief plot information was checked against the linked distributor and publisher pages. Cover and publicity images belong to their respective rights holders and are reproduced here to identify the works.

Language and exercise design draw on Ready for C1 Advanced, fourth edition: SB pp. 24–25 and 198, WB pp. 18–19; the learner’s supplied phrase bank and vocabulary list. No textbook review has been reproduced in full.

Suggested use: do one short round, then return on another day and try recall before revealing hints. The trainer does not send reminders or claim to measure long-term retention. No typing is required. Task 2 offers text selection or multiple choice. Task 3 offers phrase chunks or individual words, meaning cues, first-part hints and optional source context.

Your answers and scores are stored on this browser when permitted. Export a backup to keep them separately. No external services are required to run the exercises; images are embedded.

Title notes: Things Fall Apart is «Пришло разрушение». The second book is interpreted as The Letter Carrier (La portalettere, «Почтальонша») by Francesca Giannone. Film 1 is It Ends with Us. The Ibelin documentary’s English release title is The Remarkable Life of Ibelin.
